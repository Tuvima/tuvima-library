# Dashboard (MediaEngine.Web) — Working Rules

> Loaded automatically when Claude works on files under `src/MediaEngine.Web/`. Root `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/rules/` still apply (Dashboard product-surface rules: [`.claude/rules/dashboard-surfaces.md`](../../.claude/rules/dashboard-surfaces.md)). Per-surface product behaviour (cards, Home, details, TV, Collections, player panels) lives in [`docs/product/presentation-rules.md`](../../docs/product/presentation-rules.md) — read the relevant section before changing a surface.

## Dashboard quality gates

- Listen playback stays under `PlaybackSessionController` and the persistent native audio host; video under `VideoPlaybackHost`. Both draw one bar through `PlaybackShell` (unified player v2): identity zone, five transport buttons with Play at true center, and the tools from `PlaybackControlCatalog` (24 px stroke glyphs from `PlaybackUtilityGlyph` in 44 px targets; no Material icons in the player, guarded by `PlayerGuardrailTests`). Every tool opens a `PlaybackPanel` (menu 272, panel 400, wide 560; one open at a time through `PlaybackTransientToolCoordinator`; bottom sheets on phones and touch tablets). Speed (`PlaybackSpeedPanel`) and Sleep timer (`SleepTimerPanel`, audiobooks only) are their own tools. Desktop Now playing is the page `/listen/now-playing` (`NowPlayingPage`); there is no pop-out window. On a phone (`PlaybackFormFactor.IsPhone`: phone form factor or width 720 px or less) the bar is 56 px and expands into `PlaybackFullPlayer`, with one row of at most four tools and no volume; phone players have Collapse and no session-stop Close. Play on another device is a row in the item menu (music, audiobooks) or Playback settings (video), backed by `PlaybackRemoteController`. Close saves guarded paused resume before stopping. Controls per media are listed in `clients/contracts/v1/player-controls.md`, which `PlayerControlsContractTests` keep in step with the catalog. Follow [playback architecture](../../docs/architecture/playback.md) and the spec `engineering/specs/archive/unified-player-v2.md`; do not infer runtime acceptance from source-only checks.
- When product concepts, navigation, editing flows, data-store lifecycle, Docker startup, or CI checks change, update README/docs/AGENTS/CLAUDE in the same change (trigger table: `.claude/rules/docs.md`).
- Verification cost control: while iterating, check structure and computed styles with text tools (page text, accessibility tree, computed CSS). Take screenshots only for before/after proof at the required sizes, and keep them in ignored `.tmp/`.
- Visual checks target the four form factors (phone under 600, tablet 600–1023, desktop, TV) at the supported widths; new CSS breakpoints only 600 / 840 / 1180 (`docs/reference/ui-supported-devices.md`).

## Icon rows and player typography

Validate icons together whenever they share a row or area. Peer actions must use the same icon family, view box, stroke treatment, rendered glyph size, target size, shape, border, and alignment. Do not mix filled Material and outline playback icons within one peer row. Selected states may change color; intentionally dominant transport Play/Pause may have a larger target and must be documented as a separate role. Icon-only controls require a tooltip and accessible name, plus keyboard focus and selected/expanded semantics.

Favorite, Like and Dislike sit in the identity zone (`PlaybackIdentityActions`): 44 px targets with 24 px `PlaybackUtilityGlyph` icons in a 24-unit view box; on the phone's expanded player they keep 44 px hit areas and draw a 40 px fill. Detail hero actions keep their own size contract. Run the read-only `tools/validate-player-icon-rows.js` in the rendered browser at desktop, phone and short-phone sizes; require equal dimensions and centered glyphs within 1px. Also inspect resting, focus, rated/favorited and open-menu states. Do not infer size parity from source alone. ## Native Dashboard controls and release styling

The Dashboard uses first-party `Components/Shared/App*` primitives and scoped `Services/Ui/` services. `AppPopoverHost`, `AppDialogHost` and `AppThemeProvider` are mounted by Main, Reader and SetupWizard layouts. Dialog callers use `IAppDialogService`; popup coordination belongs to `AppPopoverService`. Native dialogs preserve the editor's unchanged URL, typed results and unsaved-change interception. No toasts: see the feedback rule in engineering/design/design-system.md. Popup ownership follows the active modal or fullscreen container so a select remains usable inside an editor or player. Pages use the shared components rather than reimplementing their controls; selectors size to the selected label, then ellipsize with a full-label tooltip.

Phone primitives (shared, opt-in, desktop unchanged): `AppDialogOptions.Sheet = AppDialogSheetMode.Phone|PhoneAndTablet` presents a dialog as a bottom sheet (grip, swipe down, backdrop tap, Escape); `<AppTable CardsOn="AppTableCardsMode.Phone|PhoneAndTablet">` stacks rows as cards and needs `data-card-role="title|subtitle|value|actions|hidden"` on the header cell and every body cell of the column (CSS in `wwwroot/app.css`); `<AppRowMenu Items=... />` is the 44 px row "more" button (sheet on phones, `AppOverflowMenu` popover elsewhere). Use these instead of hover-only or drag-only row actions.

`tuvima.tokens.css` owns canonical tokens; first-party theme aliases, `native-utilities.css`, `native-structure.css`, `native-fields.css`, global `app.css` and component-isolated styles own presentation. `AppMaterialIcon` renders the pinned `AppMaterialIcons` SVG catalog; regenerate it with `python scripts/icons/generate-material-icons.py`, retaining `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
