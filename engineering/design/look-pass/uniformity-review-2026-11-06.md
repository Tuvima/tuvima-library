# UI uniformity review: type, icons, menus, filters, hero and reaction controls

> Read-only review, 2026-11-06, Fable. Code on main at `src/MediaEngine.Web/` (paths below are relative to it). Companion to the Look pass spec (`engineering/specs/look-pass.md`), the One tile design spec (Locked, unbuilt) and the near-black color decision (page #101115, nav #14151A, card #1A1B21, hover #272830, menu #2F3039), which this review takes as given.
>
> **Verified** means read in the code or measured from it this session (counts come from grep over the Dashboard's style files, vendor files excluded unless said). **Assumption** means general knowledge about Netflix or HBO Max that was not confirmed against their live apps today; third-party style guides for both apps disagree with each other, so none is treated as authoritative. Platform numbers (WCAG 2.2, Material 3, Apple HIG) were confirmed by search.

## 0. The short answer

The Look pass rulebook is largely right. The ladder (12/13/14/16/18/20/24/32), four weights, 44 px menu rows, square chips, one dialog and one menu part all match what Netflix and HBO Max do in spirit and what the platform guidelines ask for. Five things in the spec itself should change: the **button height token (42 vs 44)**, a **second "control" type ladder (13/14/15) that undercuts the main one**, **menu text weight (600 is too heavy)**, **no line-height or icon-size ladder**, and **page titles that are not 24 px anywhere**. Beyond the spec, the biggest uniformity leaks are **four tab styles**, **six filter-control sizes (24 to 44 px)**, **two icon families on one hero row**, a **Like state that is invisible on the hero**, **title sizes that vary from 21 to 64 px by page**, and **133 browser `title=` hints beside the app's own tooltip** (section 4b).

## 1. Is the spec's type, icon and menu design right?

**Type scale (spec 4.1).** Right. 14 px body on a dark screen is where Netflix sits (third-party guides put Netflix body copy at 13 to 14 px and metadata at 13; unverified) and above the WCAG floor. The 12 px floor holds in app code: the only sub-12 px literals left are in the vendored PDF viewer (`wwwroot/vendor/pdfjs/pdf_viewer.css:673,1509`). Gaps:

- **Two ladders.** `--tl-control-font-size-sm/md/lg` = 13/14/15 px (`wwwroot/tuvima.tokens.css:113-115`) feed every button (`app.css:1790`), chip (`app.css:2109`) and view tab (`Pages/ViewPage.razor.css:142`). Spec 4.1 says controls are 16 px. Result: menus are 16, buttons 14, chips 13 and the 15 px step exists nowhere in the ladder. Recommend: 36 px controls 14 px, 44 and 48 px controls 16 px; delete the 15 px token.
- **Page title is never 24 px.** `Shared/Shell/AppPageHeader.razor.css:4` 25.6 to 35 px; `Settings/Frame/SettingsPageFrame.razor.css:113` 32 to 36; `Browse/MediaBrowseShell.razor.css:71` 24.8 to 32; `Pages/ShelfGridPage.razor.css:26` 21.6 to 28.8; `Pages/SearchPage.razor.css:25` 32 to 45; `Pages/ForMe.razor.css:22` 36 to 64; `Pages/ViewContentPage.razor.css:42` 29 to 39. 145 font sizes at or above 22 px, almost all `clamp()`. Recommend two tokens only: page title 24 px (28 at 1180 and up) and hero title 32 px (one `clamp` for the Home hero alone).
- **Line heights.** Tokens define 1.25/1.5/1.625 (`tokens.css:109-111`), but 78 distinct values are in use; the top five are 1, 1.5, 1.2, 1.4 and 1.35, and the tokens are used 6 times in total. Recommend a three-step ladder in the spec: 1.2 titles, 1.3 controls and menu rows, 1.5 body; nothing else.
- **Weights.** Spec says 400/500/600/700. In use: 700 ×222, 600 ×205, 500 ×38, 400 ×20, **800 ×26 in 18 files** (`MediaHub/MediaSectionShell.razor.css:55,605`, `MediaTiles/MediaTile.razor.css:55`, `Navigation/TopNavAccountMenu.razor.css`, `Details/HeroMetadataPills.razor.css` and others). 700 is the most common weight, which is why everything shouts. Netflix Sans ships regular/medium/bold/black (Dalton Maag, verified) and Netflix uses heavy weights only on hero titles (assumption). Recommend: 400 body, 500 labels, menu rows and chips, 600 buttons and card titles, 700 page and hero titles only; ban 800.
- **Uppercase labels.** 112 `text-transform: uppercase` rules (Details 12, Pages 7, Shared 6, Collections 5) at 12 px with letter spacing from 0.02 to 0.18 em (15 distinct values). Neither reference app uses small-caps eyebrows (assumption). Recommend one "overline" style (12 px, 600, 0.06 em) or, better, drop uppercase for section headers and use 20 px/600 sentence case like a shelf title.

**Icons (spec 4.7).** The tone rule is right but barely used (section 3). The spec gives one icon size (22 px in menus) and no ladder; Material icons are drawn on a 24 px grid, so 22 slightly blurs them. Recommend an icon ladder in the spec: 16 inline/chip, 20 in 36 px buttons, 24 in 44/48 px buttons, menus and toolbars, 32 dialog and tile icon tiles, 40 player glyphs (the player keeps its own documented sizes). Change the menu icon to 24.

**Menus (spec 4.4).** 44 px rows and 16 px text are right (Material 3 list rows are 48 to 56 dp; both reference apps use roughly 48 px rows on desktop, assumption). Gaps: rows are **600 weight** (`app.css:2878`, `TopNavAccountMenu.razor.css:130`), which reads as a list of buttons; both reference apps use regular or medium (assumption). Recommend 500. One tile spec D13 already asks for 8 px inset, 48 px rows, 14 px icon gap; the phone sheet gives 48 px rows (`app.css:5883`, `app.css:4152`), the same as desktop, not "larger" as spec 4.4 says. Recommend 52 px phone rows. Danger red `#EF4444` on the menu surface `#2F3039` is **3.48:1** (computed), below 4.5:1 for 16 px text; use a lighter red for text on dark (`#F87171` or similar; verify in the token).

**Dialogs (spec 4.3).** The build drifted from the spec: icon tile 64 px with a 30 px icon (`Shared/AppDialogShell.razor.css:42-48`) versus 44 px in the spec; close X 44 px with a 22 px icon (`:95-107`) versus 36 px; the large title is `clamp(24-32px)` (`:33`). Pick one and align both; 44 px tile and 44 px close are the better numbers for touch.

**Color on the new grays (computed).** Every text token passes AA on every new surface: primary 16.0:1, secondary 9.6:1, muted 6.7:1 on the card gray. `#8852FC` as text or icon color is 3.8:1 on the card and 2.9:1 on the menu surface; `--tl-tone-accent` already points at the hover purple `#A46FFF` (5.2:1), so purple text and icons must use that token, never the fill color. White on the purple fill is 4.51:1 (passes, no margin; do not lighten the fill). `--tl-text-faint` (0.34 alpha) is 1.9:1 and `--tl-text-disabled` 3.2:1; faint has 6 uses and should go.

## 2. Mobile specifics

- **Minimum text:** 12 px holds (verified). Keep 14 body, 13 secondary, 12 only for metadata; the Places toolbar's 0.76 rem (12.2 px) button text (`Pages/ViewPlacesPage.razor.css:8`) is the one borderline case.
- **Touch targets.** WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 (AA) is 24 × 24 CSS px; 2.5.5 (AAA), Apple HIG and Material 3 say 44 pt / 48 dp. The app has two tokens, `--tl-tap-target-min: 44px` (104 uses) and `--tl-touch-target-min: 48px` (45 uses) (`tokens.css:32-33`). Below 44 on touch today (verified in CSS, not on a device): tile "+" 32 px and "..." 36 px (`app.css:840,843`; the One tile spec D17 fixes the "..."), `AppChip` 24 px (`app.css:2102`), browse grouping chip 28 px (`app.css:2121`), `AppToggleChip` 34 px (44 only under `pointer: coarse`, `Shared/AppToggleChip.razor.css:5,21`), view kind tabs 36 px (`Pages/ViewPage.razor.css:135`), account avatar button 34 px (`Navigation/TopNavAccountMenu.razor.css:48-50`), Places toolbar 40.8 px (`ViewPlacesPage.razor.css:5`). Only 15 `pointer: coarse` and 5 `hover: none` queries exist, so most touch sizing is per component. Recommend one rule in the shared tap-target block: every interactive element is 44 px on coarse pointers, chips included; tablets count as touch (the design system already says so).
- **Bottom sheets vs popovers.** The shared sheet exists (`AppDialogSheetMode`) and is opted into in 15 places; `AppRowMenu` (42 uses) and the detail/tile "..." (`Details/OverflowActionMenu.razor:14`) become sheets on phones, the browse filters too (`Browse/BrowseFilterSheet.razor`). Bare `AppOverflowMenu` (28 uses) stays a popover on a phone, and the Places page draws its own anchored filter panel (`ViewPlacesPage.razor.css:9`). Both reference apps use a bottom sheet for every "more" menu and for sort/filter on phones (assumption). Recommend: the one menu part is a sheet on phones by default; no page opts in.
- **Filter chip rows.** Phone section chips are 44 px and scroll with an edge fade (`Navigation/PhoneSectionChips.razor.css`), which is the right pattern (Netflix mobile shows a scrolling chip row under the top bar; assumption). Browse filters are a different 42 px toggle (`MediaBrowseShell.razor.css:412`) and 42 px quick filter (`Browse/AppQuickFilterToggle.razor.css:7`); View uses a chip row of `AppChip` at 24 px (`View/ViewFilterChipRow.razor.css`). Recommend one filter chip: 36 px desktop, 44 touch, 14 px/500, square 3 px, selected = purple soft fill with purple border, one "Clear" chip last.
- **Tab strips.** The shared tab is 44 px with 18 px labels (`app.css:308,316`), but phone detail tabs are a grid with a 112 px minimum (`app.css:5898-5903`), View kind tabs are a segmented pill (`ViewPage.razor.css:126-168`), hero section tabs use 12 px labels (`Cinematic/SurfaceTabBar.razor.css:35`), and Browse restyles `.tl-tab` (`Browse/BrowseShellStyles.razor.css:112-140`, the named exception). Recommend 16 px tab labels (18 is larger than the body copy under them) and one segmented control only for Grid/List.
- **Tile density.** The One tile spec fixes 112 × 168 phone posters (three across), which matches Netflix mobile (assumption). Continue tiles are 72 vw on phones (`MediaTiles/ContinueLandscapeTile.razor.css:20`), a sensible peek-the-next-one width.
- **Breakpoints.** The rule says 600/840/1180 only, but 27 rules break at 720, 30 at 640, 12 at 900 and 10 at 1100 (verified counts). The guardrail covers new CSS only.

## 3. Icons: one set?

Nearly. Verified counts:

| Family | Uses | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Material **Outlined** (pinned SVG catalog, `AppMaterialIcon`) | 1,876 | everywhere |
| Material **Filled** (same catalog) | 267 | 44 files mix Filled and Outlined |
| `PlaybackUtilityGlyph` (hand-drawn 1.5 px stroke lines, 24 grid) | 45 | player, dock, **detail hero companions and tile "..."** |
| FontAwesome Solid via `AppIcon` | 2 files | `AppIconTile`, `DetailHero` |
| Inline `<svg>` | 16 files | charts, avatars, spinner, pin pad (mostly legitimate) |

So: one catalog, **three drawn styles** (filled glyph, outlined glyph, thin stroke line), mixed on the same row. On the detail hero the primary button carries a 24 px Material icon while the companion buttons carry 22 px stroke glyphs (`Details/HeroActionRow.razor:15,26`; sizes `HeroActionRow.razor.css:6,18`). Sizes have no ladder: the only size classes are 20 and 36 px (`wwwroot/native-structure.css:9-10`); literals of 16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 28, 29, 32, 34 and 68 px exist. Color rules: `AppIconTone` exists (11 tones) but `Tone=` appears **4 times** and `AppIconTile` **6 times** in all; 11 CSS rules color icons directly, 4 with a hex. Recommend: Material Outlined everywhere, Filled only for a selected or on state (both reference apps fill the thumb/check when selected; assumption), retire FontAwesome, keep the stroke glyphs only inside the player where their row contract is documented, and add the icon-size ladder from section 1 as tokens.

## 4. Hero actions, reactions, tile and detail icon buttons (scope addition)

What the code draws (verified): primary hero button 48 px tall, 11 rem minimum width, purple fill with a 1 px 30 percent white border, 18 px/600 text, 24 px icon, 10 px corners (`HeroActionRow.razor.css:4`); Resume shows "NN% watched" inline at 14 px/400 with a 3 px bar along the bottom (`:16-17,8-9`); companion buttons are 48 px rounded squares with a 1.5 px 55 percent white border on 30 percent black, 22 px stroke glyph, border turns purple on hover (`:5-7`); on phones the primary stretches to 22 rem and each companion gets a word under it (`app.css:5872`). Reaction: one thumb button, 48 px in the hero and 44 px elsewhere; hover or focus opens a two-choice flyout above it (Not for me, I like this), each 44 px, selected choice gets the menu-selected wash (`Shared/MediaRateControl.razor.css:2-4,9`). Tiles: "+" is a 32 px square and "..." a 36 px square, shown on hover and always on touch, with a 3 px white outline plus 6 px purple glow on hover (`app.css:837-844`); the saved "+" turns purple.

How the references do it (assumptions unless noted): Netflix's hero Play is a white filled rounded rectangle with black bold text and a filled triangle; secondary actions ("More info", "My List") are translucent gray; the thumb hover reveals three choices (Not for me, I like this, Love this; verified from press coverage of the two-thumbs feature) and the chosen thumb fills; icon buttons are circles with a thin border. HBO Max's Play is also white filled, with rounded-rectangle secondaries; its like control is a single heart/plus style "My List" rather than thumbs.

Findings:

1. **Selected Like is invisible on the hero.** `data-rated` is styled only in the player dock (`Listen/PlaybackSongActions.razor.css:9`); the hero trigger uses the stroke glyph with no `Filled` and no color change (`MediaRateControl.razor:5`, `HeroActionRow.razor:26`). A liked title looks unliked until you hover. Recommend: filled glyph plus accent border when rated, everywhere.
2. **Two icon styles on one row** (section 3). Pick Material for the whole hero row.
3. **Purple fill plus white border** is neither reference pattern; Shy chose purple fill, so keep it, but drop the border (the fill is the shape). Companion buttons' 30 percent black fill is right over artwork; add the same on the primary's hover (currently inherited from `AppButton`?  not verified).
4. **Flyout on hover only.** On touch the thumb opens the flyout on tap (pointer type check, `MediaRateControl.razor:31`), fine; but the flyout sits above the button and can leave the hero on short phones (not verified on a device). Recommend a bottom sheet with the two (or three) choices on phones, through the one menu part.
5. **Tile hover is loud.** 3 px white plus 6 px purple glow conflicts with One tile D5 (thin 2 px purple outline, no ring); the "+" goes away under D4. Nothing to add beyond building that spec.
6. **Sizes are good:** 48 primary, 48 companions, 44 flyout choices, 36 "..." (44 tap area pending D17). Keep.

## 4b. The app's own tooltip (scope addition)

What exists (verified): `Shared/AppTooltip.razor` (47 uses) wraps a control and hands it to `wwwroot/js/playback-tooltip.js`, which already does most of the hard part well: the bubble is a `popover="manual"` element in the browser's top layer, appended inside an open modal dialog when there is one so nothing can cover it (`playback-tooltip.js:5-9`); it is centered above the anchor with an 8 px gap, flips below when there is no room, and is clamped to the visual viewport (`:46-50`); it shows on keyboard focus (`:focus-visible` only), hides on Escape, blur, scroll, resize and when the control's menu opens, and sets `aria-describedby` (`:33-41,52-54`). Hover waits **400 ms** (`:51`); touch never hovers, a press starts a **600 ms** timer and release hides it (`:54`). The look is borrowed from the player: `.playback-tooltip` (`app.css:2964-2969`) uses the player surface and border, 6 px corners, 7 × 10 px padding, **12 px** text at 1.35 line height, a soft shadow, width capped at the viewport minus 16 px. The `Arrow` parameter is accepted and ignored (no data attribute, no CSS). Meanwhile **133 browser `title=` hints in 85 Razor files** (Shared 24, Details 20, Settings 21, Listen 4, Pages 3, Browse 2) bypass it, 21 of them through `AppNativeButton`'s own `Title` parameter (`Shared/AppNativeButton.razor:7,23`). Four are legitimate (`iframe`, `img`, `abbr`, `time` accessibility names). There is no guardrail.

Recommended design (one part, one token set, renamed `AppTooltip`/`--tl-tooltip-*` since it is not a player thing):

- **Look.** Background the menu surface (`#2F3039` in the near-black set) with the subtle border, 6 px corners, 6 × 10 px padding, **13 px / 500** text in `--tl-text-primary`, line height 1.3, max width 280 px (wraps to two lines, never three; longer text belongs in a dialog or the docs), shadow `--tl-shadow-md`, **no arrow** (Netflix and HBO Max show plain small boxes without a caret; assumption). Delete the `Arrow` parameter. Contrast: primary text on `#2F3039` is 12.2:1.
- **Timing.** Near-instant: show after **150 ms** on hover, **0 ms** on keyboard focus; hide at once on leave. While any tooltip was shown in the last 300 ms, the next sibling's shows with no delay (the "warm" rule both reference apps and the OS use; assumption for the apps, verified behavior on Windows and macOS menus in general). Fade in 100 ms, respect reduce-motion.
- **Placement.** Above the anchor, centered, 8 px gap, flip below when clipped, clamp to the viewport, always in the top layer and inside the open dialog (keep today's logic). Only `Top` and `Bottom` placements; drop `Left`/`Right` options to keep one look.
- **Keyboard.** Show on `:focus-visible`, hide on Escape without closing anything else (Escape is consumed by the tooltip only when one is showing), keep `aria-describedby`; the accessible name (`aria-label`) stays on the control, the tooltip never carries information that exists nowhere else.
- **Touch.** No hover. For icon-only controls, a **long press (500 ms)** shows the tooltip and keeps it for 1.5 s after release so it can be read, and the press does not fire the click (today the release hides it instantly, so a thumb covers the text and it vanishes on lift). For controls with a visible label, nothing shows. Tablets follow the pointer type, not the form factor.
- **Guardrail.** A `TooltipGuardrailTests` that fails on `title="` in any Razor file outside an allowlist of `img`, `iframe`, `abbr`, `time` and SVG `<title>`; retire `AppNativeButton.Title` (callers wrap in `AppTooltip` or pass `Tooltip` to `AppIconButton`); a second assertion that every `AppIconButton` has `Tooltip` or `AriaLabel` already exists and stays.

## 5. Top 16 inconsistencies across View, Settings, Home, Browse, Details, Editor (ranked by user impact)

1. Page titles differ on every page (21 to 64 px) while the spec says 24 (section 1).
2. Four tab styles: underline tabs (Settings, Plugins), segmented pill (View kinds), 12 px hero section tabs, Browse pill switcher (section 2).
3. Six filter-control sizes: 24 chip, 28 grouping chip, 34 toggle chip, 42 browse toggle, 42 quick filter, 44 phone chip; three selected-state looks (section 2).
4. Like state invisible on the hero but visible in the dock (section 4).
5. Buttons 14 px, menus 16 px, chips 13 px: the second control ladder (section 1).
6. 700 and 800 weights on section labels and metadata make headings and body compete; 26 uses of 800.
7. Menu rows at 600 weight versus menu-like rows elsewhere (Settings nav rows, account menu header at 700, `TopNavAccountMenu.razor.css:102`).
8. Icon styles mixed on one row (hero, tile "..." vs tile "+") and 44 files mixing Filled and Outlined.
9. Touch targets below 44 on chips, tile buttons, view tabs and the avatar button (section 2).
10. Phone menus: sheet for row menus and the detail "...", popover for bare overflow menus and the Places filters.
11. Dialog build drifted from spec: 64 px icon tile and 44 px X versus 44 and 36 (section 1).
12. Uppercase eyebrows with 15 letter-spacing values versus sentence-case shelf titles on Home.
13. Line heights: 78 values; tight 1 and 1.15 on multi-line text in places.
14. Danger red fails contrast as 16 px menu text on the menu surface (3.48:1).
15. Two kinds of hint: 47 app tooltips (12 px, player-styled, 400 ms) beside 133 browser `title=` hints that look different in every browser and never show on touch or keyboard (section 4b).
16. Focus: `a:focus-visible, button:focus { outline: none }` (`wwwroot/native-utilities.css:7`) with per-part replacements; some controls lose the ring (not verified on screen). Also 774 hex colors and 1,324 forced overrides remain in component styles (verified counts; down from 958 and 1,504).

## 6. Proposed amendments to the Look pass spec (work packets)

Each is an addendum to section 4; none re-opens a decision Shy has made.

- **A1 Control height and type (Mechanical).** Set `--tl-control-height-md` to 44 (`tokens.css:328`); delete `--tl-control-font-size-lg`; 36 px controls 14 px, 44/48 px controls 16 px; menu text 500. Depends on: none. Done when: a token test asserts 36/44/48 and the two font steps; buttons measure 44/16 on the preview page.
- **A2 Title and line-height tokens (Standard).** Add `--tl-font-size-title` (24, 28 at 1180+) and keep `-hero` for the Home hero only; add line-height ladder 1.2/1.3/1.5; replace the 145 large `clamp()` sizes and ratchet line heights to the three tokens. Depends on: A1. Done when: no page title outside the two tokens; line-height ratchet at 3 values plus a player allowlist.
- **A3 Weights and labels (Mechanical).** Ban 800; 700 for titles only; one overline style or sentence-case section headers; retire `--tl-text-faint`. Depends on: A2. Done when: weight ratchet has zero 800 and the uppercase count falls below 20.
- **A4 Icon ladder and one family (Standard).** Icon size tokens 16/20/24/32/40; menu icon 24; Material Outlined by default, Filled only for selected states (guardrail: a Filled icon needs a selected or on condition in the same markup); retire FontAwesome `AppIcon`; stroke glyphs allowlisted to the player; wire `AppIconTone` into `AppMaterialIcon` so a page passes a tone. Depends on: none. Done when: IconToneGuardrail counts tone use above 50 and FontAwesome has zero users.
- **A5 One filter chip and one tab (Standard).** `AppChip` kind "filter": 36 px desktop, 44 touch, 14/500, square, one selected look; replace browse toggles, quick filters, View chips and Places filter buttons; `.tl-tab` label 16 px; View kind tabs and Browse switcher become `AppSegmentedControl` (Grid/List only). Depends on: A1. Done when: tab guardrail allowlist is empty except the segmented control; no filter control under 44 on coarse pointers.
- **A6 Phone sheets by default (Standard).** The one menu part is a sheet on phones with 52 px rows; bare `AppOverflowMenu`, the Places filter panel and the thumb flyout use it. Depends on: WP3. Done when: no popover opens on a phone-width page in the preview page's menu list.
- **A7 Hero and reaction states (Standard).** Rated state filled plus accent border on every surface; Material icons on the whole hero row; drop the primary's white border; reaction choices in a sheet on phones. Depends on: A4, A6. Done when: a rated title shows the filled thumb at rest in hero, tile menu and dock (bUnit assertion), one icon family in `HeroActionRow`.
- **A8 Dialog anatomy fix (Mechanical).** Icon tile 44, X 44 with 24 px icon, large title only for setup. Depends on: WP2. Done when: DialogAnatomyGuardrail asserts the sizes.
- **A9 Touch floor (Standard).** Shared coarse-pointer rule: all interactive elements 44 px minimum (chips, avatar button, tile buttons, Places toolbar); one touch token (retire `--tl-touch-target-min` or make it an alias). Depends on: A5. Done when: `tools/validate-touch-targets.js` reports zero at 360 and 768 on Home, Watch, View, Settings, a detail page and the editor.
- **A10 Contrast and focus (Mechanical).** Danger text token for dark surfaces (4.5:1 on `#2F3039`); accent text and icons always `--tl-tone-accent`; remove the global outline reset and rely on the ring tokens. Depends on: none. Done when: a contrast test over the token pairs passes; the focus ring shows on every control in the preview page.
- **A11 Tooltip (Standard).** Rename `playback-tooltip` to `app-tooltip` with `--tl-tooltip-*` tokens (surface, text 13/500, radius 6, max width 280, shadow); 150 ms hover, 0 ms focus, 300 ms warm window, no arrow, Top/Bottom only; touch long-press 500 ms with a 1.5 s linger and no click; replace the 133 `title=` hints (wrap in `AppTooltip`, or `Tooltip=` on `AppIconButton`), retire `AppNativeButton.Title`; add `TooltipGuardrailTests` with the four-element allowlist. Files: `Shared/AppTooltip.razor`, `wwwroot/js/playback-tooltip.js`, `app.css:2961-2969`, the 85 Razor files, `Shared/AppNativeButton.razor`. Depends on: A10 (surface tokens). Done when: the guardrail passes with the allowlist only; a bUnit test shows the tooltip on focus and none on `pointerType: touch` hover; the design preview page shows one tooltip look.
- **A12 Visual proof addendum (Standard).** Add to WP15: a before/after of one Home shelf, one Settings page, the View places toolbar, the detail hero and a tooltip on each at 360, 768 and 1440. Depends on: A1 to A11.

Order: A1, A4, A10 together (foundation); then A2, A5, A6, A11; then A3, A7, A8, A9; A12 last. A1 and A8 touch files WP2 to WP4 own, so they go to whoever holds those packets.

## Plain English summary

The rulebook is sound: one ladder of text sizes, four weights, 44 px menu rows, square chips, one dialog and one menu, all of which is how the big streaming apps work and what the accessibility guidelines ask for. Where the app still feels uneven is in the gaps the rulebook left open: buttons sit on a second, smaller text ladder; page titles are a different size on every page; menu text is bold; icons come in three drawing styles and are mixed on the same row; filters come in six heights; tabs in four styles; and a title you have liked does not look liked on its own page. Phones are mostly right (12 px floor, 44 px chips, sheets for row menus) but several small controls are still under the 44 px finger size and some menus still open as little popovers. Hints are split between the app's own tooltip (already well built: it floats above everything, works from the keyboard, waits a little too long) and 133 browser hints that look different in every browser and never show on a phone; one tooltip with a quicker show, a long-press on touch and a check that stops new browser hints closes that. The twelve amendments above close those gaps without reopening anything already decided, and most are mechanical once the tokens are in place.
