People inside the Match tab

Draft mock-ups for Editor v9, version 2 (issue #1244). Change from version 1: People becomes a third switch inside Match, after Media providers and Wikidata, which both stay instead of its own tab, it works on titles that have no Wikidata page of their own (example: a MasterClass video folder), it copes with long lists, and the person's own page has no "More" button. Dark only. Example: the video folder "MasterClass: Stephen Curry" whose host was matched to the wrong Stephen Curry.

A. Ways in, and the person page

Left: the title's Cast and crew, unchanged. Right: a person's page.

  1. 1Cast and crew stays exactly as it is today: no new buttons or menu entries. You spot the wrong person here, then edit the title.
  2. 2Edit in the title's "..." menu (not drawn) opens the editor; Match, then People is the only way in.
  3. 3The person's own page has no "More" button and no edit entry. Looked-up people are treated as right; fixes happen on the title.

B. Match tab, People switch

Desktop 1280. A video folder with no Wikidata page: the Match switch keeps Media providers and Wikidata and adds People.

  1. 1Match switch: Media providers and Wikidata stay as they are; People is added after them. It is shown for any title with people, whether or not the title itself is matched.
  2. 2Find a person filters the list by name or role, so a long cast is quick to scan.
  3. 3Rows: headshot, name from the file or provider, role, who it is matched to, and a chip. "Check" is a hint only, here because a basketball class lists a person Wikidata knows as a politician.
  4. 4Change person on a row opens screen C. Show all 41 reveals the rest, a page at a time.

C. Change person

Same step as before. With no Wikidata page for the title, results are judged by name, role and description instead of "lists this title".

  1. 1"This title" bar names the video folder and the credit being changed.
  2. 2Results carry Wikidata's one-line description so you can pick the basketball player yourself. Current is tagged.
  3. 3The card compares name and role. The "lists this title" row says the title has no Wikidata page, and the verdict is honest: "Could not confirm".
  4. 4Use this person stages it. Roles stay as pulled from the retail listing (a person can have several, e.g. Guest · Writer) and are not edited here. Only this title changes. Links to the person's Wikidata and Wikipedia pages are on the card and on every row; search finds people only.

D. Staged, then Review changes

Unchanged from version 1.

  1. 1Row shows the new person with "Not saved yet" and Undo.
  2. 2Review changes says this title only, and the choice is kept through refreshes.

E. On a phone

360 wide. Match shows the three switches as a scrolling row.

  1. 1People switch in Match; filter box above the rows.
  2. 2Change person is full width under a flagged row.
  3. 3The result card is a full-height sheet with Use this person at the bottom.
