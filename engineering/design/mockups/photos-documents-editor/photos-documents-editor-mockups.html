Photos, videos and documents editor

Built on the Locked editor v4 frame, so it looks and behaves like every other editor. Each screen shows exactly what the Dashboard would show at 1440 px wide. Yellow numbers mark the parts explained underneath. Pictures are stand-in color blocks.
