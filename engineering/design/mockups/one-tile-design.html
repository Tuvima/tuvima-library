  1. "..." menu, top right, always visible. A vertical three-dot mark on every tile, no box around it. It holds the actions that apply: Add to My List, Restart, Open Universe, Edit, Delete, and on Continue watching, Go to show and Remove from Continue.
  2. Play triangle at the bottom left of a Continue tile's picture.
  3. The only tile text: Continue watching tiles show the title (or "S3 E8 Episode title") and then the rating and year under the picture. A progress bar runs along the bottom edge of the picture.
  4. Hover outline: a thin purple line (the Library purple) around the cover only. The tile does not grow, move or change shape.
  5. No "+" button. Add to My List is the first row of the "..." menu on every device. The hover state is the outline alone.
  6. Status label, top left (New, New episode): a small dark label flush to the corner of the cover, touching both edges, not floating inside it.
  7. No text under the cover. The title is in the cover art itself.
  8. Other videos in a folder, no cover art: same poster-size tile; a landscape still on top, the folder name and its count ("3 videos") in text at the bottom.
  9. Mixed rows: an album sits beside posters at exactly the same height (321 px), so it is wider (321 px). Rows always line up, which matters on Recently added where music and movies appear together.
  10. Bottom label, flush to the bottom-left corner: for a Series, Universe or album, the number inside it ("6 books"; the Dune Saga tile is a Series, and its name sits on the art like any cover). The same slot will hold Top 10 and similar later. It no longer shares a corner with the New label.
