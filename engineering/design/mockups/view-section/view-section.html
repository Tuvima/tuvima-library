Notes: Photos page

  1. 1Recently added is one more pill. It sorts the whole page by the day each photo was added, newest first, so an old photo you imported today is at the top.
  2. 2On this day sits above the timeline on the All view. Each card is one past year with photos from today's date; tapping opens those photos. It draws from Mine and the Shared Library only, never from Vault or other people's private photos, so nothing is logged as a look at someone else. It shows on the All pill only and hides itself on days with nothing.
  3. 3The Scope and Density selects are gone from the top. Density now lives inside More filters, so the page opens with only the title and the pills.
  4. 4Viewing: Mine is the one place you choose whose library you are in. It sits at the top of the left rail and applies to every page under it (Photos, Galleries, Documents, People, Places). The "Personal" group heading is gone because the page is not always personal now.

Notes: switching whose library, and a Shared Library photo

  1. 1Opening the switch lists Mine, the Shared Library, then each household member you may look at. Choosing one changes every page at once. Looking at another person is read-only.

Notes: tile menu

  1. 1The tile "..." menu gets the same action list as the selection bar and the viewer: Favorite, Add to Gallery, Download, Edit, Archive, Trash. Today it only has Edit and Trash, so one photo means select first, then act.
  2. 2The menu opens in the top layer above the grid, so it is never clipped by the tile or the page edge. Trash asks "Are you sure?" once, then happens at once. On phones the menu opens as a bottom sheet and has no Edit and no Trash. Controls are 44 px tall and icons are Material, Filled only when selected. No browser hover text; any hint uses the app's own instant tooltip.

Notes: dropping files onto Photos

  1. 1While files upload, the slim ribbon under the pills shows how many are done and a progress bar. You can keep browsing; the ribbon closes itself when the upload finishes and shows "5 photos added" for a moment.
  2. 2Anything that is not a photo or video is refused by name with the reason. The valid files from the same drop still upload. The message stays until you close it.
  3. 3You can drop anywhere on the page, over the pills, the timeline or empty space. While you drag, a dashed outline appears around the whole page. It accepts photos and videos; a document dropped here gets the refusal above.
