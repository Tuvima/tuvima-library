TV player: panels, paused details and end card

For approval (spec native-apps, WP2 and D3). One layout for every television: TV web (Samsung and LG), Android TV and Fire TV, Roku and Apple TV. Each picture is a 1920 × 1080 screen showing exactly the words, order and defaults of clients/contracts/v1/player-controls.md. Orange numbers match the notes under each picture. Dark only.

What changes from today: nothing in the words or the order. This fixes the layout the contract called a "first definition": panel width, where each part sits, and what is focused first. Once approved, the contract's Televisions section is rewritten to match.

  • 1. Controls overlay (TV episode) · 2. Paused details · 3. Episodes panel · 4. Audio & subtitles panel · 5. Bookmarks panel · 6. Playback settings panel · 7. End card · 8. Find subtitles window · 9. Remote focus rules per device

9. Remote focus rules per device

The same behaviour on every TV; only the physical keys differ.

BehaviourTV web (Samsung, LG)Android TV / Fire TVRokuApple TV
Show overlayAny key. Hides after 5 seconds without input, never while a panel is open.
Move focusArrows. Up and Down move timeline, transport row, tool row. Left and Right on the timeline skip by the profile's seconds.
PressOK / EnterD-pad centreOKClick on the touch surface
BackReturn key; closes the panel first, then hides the overlayBack; same orderBack; same orderMenu; same order
Skip keysRewind and Fast-forwardRewind and Fast-forward where the remote has them; the transport row always covers themRewind and Fast-forward (skip buttons)Swipe on the touch surface moves focus; skip buttons on screen
Panel focusOpens on the current row (Episodes), the selected row (Audio & subtitles), the Bookmark-at button (Bookmarks) or the first row (Playback settings). Right moves from the list to the panel; Left returns.
Focus ringWhite ring plus a lighter fill; the ring is always visible. Controls never move when something is dimmed.
Text sizeRows at least 56 px high at 1080p and body text no smaller than 24 px, checked from the sofa on the real Roku.
