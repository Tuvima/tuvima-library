H. Window shape on a wide screen (#981)

A 2560 × 1440 screen, drawn to scale. Not part of the page bottom, but it changes how wide the page below is.

Today: 1440 × 1104, nearly square. The hero is tall and narrow (about 1.3 : 1), so a landscape background loses its sides
Proposed: 1920 × 1104. The hero is about 1.8 : 1, close to the 16 : 9 picture, so very little is cut off
  1. ·Today the width stops at 1440 but the height keeps growing with the screen, so a landscape background is cropped. The proposal lets the width keep growing with the height up to 1920, with the page still visible around the window.
