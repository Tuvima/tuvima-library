Unified player v2

One player shell for music, audiobooks, TV and movies. Each screen shows exactly what appears in the app; the purple numbers are reference markers for the notes underneath and are not part of the design.

1. The persistent bar for each kind of media (desktop, 1280 wide)

2. Hover, tooltip and volume (music bar)

3. Audio & subtitles (TV and movies)

4. Subtitle appearance (nested view)

5. Playback settings and Speed

6. Sleep timer (audiobooks)

7. Lyrics (music, over the library)

8. Queue (music): Up next and History

9. Episodes (TV)

10. Chapters (audiobooks)

11. Bookmarks (every media with bookmarks)

12. Item actions menu (music)

13. Now playing (music)

14. Video paused

15. Phone

16. Lock screen, Play on another device, TV and car
