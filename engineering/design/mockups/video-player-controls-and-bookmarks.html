Video player controls and bookmarks

Mockups for Shy's review, drawn at 1440 px wide. Every frame is the real player size scaled down, so icon sizes and spacing are true to what you would see on your monitor. Layout follows the reference bar you sent: play first, the episode name centred, the tools on the right.

1. Playing, nothing hovered

The resting look. Icons are the size they used to be only on hover (34 px glyphs in 52 px targets). Bookmarks sit on the progress rail as small round bubbles: purple for yours, white for shared, blue for pack bookmarks. Chapters stay thin lines so the two never look alike.

Pausing brings the show and episode name to the centre of the picture, the way the reference does, and dims the video a little. The bar stays where it is so your hand does not have to move.

Hovering any tool turns it purple and shows its name; the Subtitles icon keeps a small purple underline whenever captions are on. Hovering Bookmarks opens the quick list; moving the mouse away closes it, and a click pins it open.

Bookmarks
  • One click to add. The purple row at the top saves a bookmark at the current time immediately, with no note. Typing in the note field first and pressing Enter saves it with the note. Keyboard: B adds a bookmark without opening anything, and a small "Bookmark added 13:42" line shows on the rail for two seconds (no toast).
  • The list is the quick list. Newest at the bottom? No: in time order, so it reads like chapters. Each row: marker colour, time, note, who it belongs to; Play jumps there; the bin deletes with an inline "Delete?" confirm. The row nearest the current time is highlighted.

  • Hover behaviour fix: a hover-opened panel always closes when the mouse leaves it; only a click pins it. Today the Speed panel pins itself by mistake (details in the spec).

A. Where Up Next goes

Recommended: fold the queue and chapters into the Episodes panel (tabs: Episodes · Chapters · Up next) so the bar has six tools. Alternative: keep a seventh icon.

B. Paused title

Recommended: large centred title over the picture when paused (frame 2). Alternative: only the bar's centre text, no big title.

C. Bookmark list order

Recommended: by time in the video, with the nearest one highlighted. Alternative: newest first, as the Saved tab does today.

D. The B key

Recommended: B adds a bookmark instantly without opening anything, Shift+B opens the list. Alternative: no shortcut.
