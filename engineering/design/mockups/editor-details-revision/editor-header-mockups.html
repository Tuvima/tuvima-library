Editor header, Details tab and Other Videos

A revision of the Locked v4 header (decision D2) and Details tab. The window is now the near-black of the details page, and the Quick Facts card takes the app's purple accent. The separate row of three dropdowns is gone. The show is a fixed tile at the left of the header. Season and Episode are two dropdowns inside the header, under the title. Everything else in the editor is unchanged from the locked mockups. Each screen is 1440 px wide unless it says otherwise. Pictures are stand-in colour blocks.

A film, a standalone book and a person keep today's header with no tile and no dropdowns.
