Screen A5 · Phone: a plain list (same on all three)

Under 600 px every song list is a simple list like Apple Music or Spotify: flat rows with no card boxes, the cover on the left (not on an album's own page, where the album art is already at the top), title with the artist beneath (no album name, so rows never wrap), and "..." on the right. Tap a row to play; press and hold or tap "..." for the bottom sheet. No columns, resizing or drag.

Part B · One reader frame

Screen B1 · The top line, bottom line and panel are one shared frame. Only what is drawn on the page, and the bottom line's contents, differ between a book and a comic or PDF.

Book (EPUB), light theme
Chapter 7

The harbor had gone quiet by the time Ada reached the pier, and the lamps along the water threw long gold lines across the swell. She counted the boats by their lights and lost count twice.

Somewhere behind her a gate clanged, and the sound carried the way sound does over water, further than it should have, and later.

"You came," said the keeper, without turning around.

She had not meant to. That was the truth of it, and the truth had a way of sounding like an excuse when spoken aloud, so she said nothing and let the tide say it for her.

The keeper set down his lantern. Nobody comes to the pier at this hour unless they are running from something or toward it.

"Which is it, then?"

  1. Title and Close. Title at the left with author (book) or Series and issue (comic) or file kind and page count (PDF). Close (X) is always the last control at the top right, in both readers and in every panel, and returns to the Library. There is no Back arrow at the left. Same size, same place.
  2. Tools, in one fixed order. Search · Contents · Pages · Bookmark this page · Bookmarks · Reading settings, then Close. A reader leaves out a tool it does not have (a book has no Pages grid; a comic has no Search or Contents; a PDF has all six). The bookmark icon fills when this page is bookmarked, and pressing it again removes it.
  3. Bottom line. The frame keeps the space and the auto-hide (it hides while you read, returns on tap or mouse move). A book shows chapter and percent; a comic or PDF shows the page slider with bookmark marks and the page count.
