PDF and comic reader

Mockups for the Locked spec (2026-11-10). Each screen shows exactly what the reader shows. The yellow numbers are not part of the product; they point at the notes under each screen. Comic pages are drawn as colored panels to stand in for artwork.

  1. Back returns to the page you came from. Your page is saved.
  2. Title, with the issue and creators under it (a PDF shows the author).
  3. Pages: opens the grid of small page pictures (screen 4).
  4. Bookmark this page. It fills in when the page on screen has your bookmark; pressing again removes it.
  5. Bookmarks: opens the list (screen 5).
  6. Settings: layout, fit, direction, background (screen 3).
  7. Click or tap the left third to go back a page, the right third to go forward. Arrow keys and Space do the same. With Right to left on, the sides swap.
  8. Click or tap the middle to show or hide the top line and the slider. They fade by themselves after 3 seconds.
  9. While you drag or hover on the slider, a small picture of that page shows above it.
  10. Bookmark marks on the slider. Off by default; they appear only when "Show bookmarks on the bar" is on.
  11. Where you are. In one-page layout it reads "Page 12 of 28".
