Look pass: one look for the whole app

Six screens, drawn exactly as the app will show them once the rules are built. Numbered dots sit on the screen; what each one means is listed underneath. Colors and shapes are the app's own; the sizes are the new standard.

Three dialogs, one anatomy. Every dialog in the app uses it.

  • 1Icon tile in the color of the situation: amber for a warning, red for something that removes things, purple for an ordinary question. Same 44 px tile everywhere.
  • 2Title in 20 px bold. A question for confirmations (“Delete Dune Part One?”), a plain label for forms.
  • 3A close X at the top right of every dialog. Closing is the same as the safe choice, never the destructive one.
  • 4Body text is 16 px, one to two short lines, saying what happens and what stays.
  • 5The safe action is always the filled purple button on the far right and has keyboard focus when the dialog opens.
  • 6The destructive action is outlined red, never filled, and sits to its left. This is the only place red is used on a button.
  • 7Delete asks once and then happens at once. “Delete” is outlined red, “Cancel” is the filled safe button.
  • 8A normal question has a secondary Cancel (outlined) beside one filled purple action.

2. Buttons

Three levels, three heights. Nothing else.

1▶ Play 2▶ Resume 3 4
5Save changes 6Cancel 7Skip for now 8Delete 9Edit
  • 1Hero Play, Read, Watch and Listen: 48 px tall, 18 px text, one line. Wide enough for its word, never cramped.
  • 2Resume is the same size as Play and says only “Resume”. Progress is a thin bar along the bottom edge of the button.
  • 3Icon buttons next to Play (My List, rate, “...”) are 48 px squares with the same corners as every control.
  • 4The hover highlight is exactly the button’s own box, so there is no bigger invisible circle.
  • 5Primary: filled purple, one per screen, 44 px.
  • 6Secondary: clearly outlined, for Back, Cancel and Check again.
  • 7Quiet: text only, for the least important choice.
  • 8Destructive: outlined red, always the less prominent of a pair.
  • 9Small (36 px) is for dense rows and tables on desktop only.
