# Playback implementation contract

Sol's WP0 decision, October 1, 2026. Implements the approved visual correction plan. The six supplied references govern player surfaces only.

## Product experience and acceptance

1. The persistent dock has a recognizable navy, rounded frame, artwork and catalog identity on the left, centered transport, and spaced tools on the right. Its popup button is directly available. Playback continues while opening catalog links. Maps to WP1/WP3/WP5.
2. Panels share aligned rows and blue/purple current states. Queue includes the current track with animated bars while playing and static bars while paused or reduced motion is requested. Audiobook history is grouped by local date. Maps to WP2/WP3/WP6.
3. Desktop Now Playing supplies large truthful artwork and media context while the persistent dock owns playback controls. Phone full players and popup players supply their own generous transport and distinct media-specific tools. All use actual artwork atmosphere. Phone layouts can scroll when necessary. Maps to WP2/WP4.
4. Popup player and its optional attached panel form a centered composition; the narrow popup becomes one column. It controls the existing audio host. Maps to WP2/WP5.
5. Video remains dominant with centered transport, lower-left identity, a full-width timeline, and lower-right settings. Changing media updates capabilities before asynchronous loading finishes. Maps to WP1/WP6.

The single audio owner, catalog metadata, source-authored chapter names, View authorization, and real available controls remain the behavioral foundation. No new Cast capability or invented counts are introduced. Acceptance requires screenshot comparison at 1920/1440/1024, phone widths 430/390/320, wide/narrow popup, plus keyboard, paused, reduced-motion and long-title checks.

## Shared visual recipe

Use a navy base (#0b1727), elevated navy (#15273b), muted blue text (#adbedb), white foreground (#f5f8ff), thin blue border (rgba(137,174,230,.24)), and blue progress (#547dff). Interaction accent, hover, focus and selection reuse the existing `--tl-accent-primary` family through the shared playback tokens. Normal controls remain white. Artwork atmosphere is a bounded medium/large rendition rendered behind a blur and navy readability overlay; a generic gradient alone is insufficient when art exists. No original rendition is used.

Desktop dock: 84–96px visual height, 12–20px radius, 56–64px art, 38–44px tool targets and 40–48px primary play. It floats with 8–16px outer space and follows the real persistent lane rail on wide lane pages. Reserve equal left/right tracks around the middle using grid 1fr minmax(280px, 36–40%) 1fr, with shrinking/truncating identity and utilities. At constrained desktop width move secondary controls into available secondary access, never shift the middle from the dock center or grow the dock beyond its height contract. Audiobook dock uses Speed, Chapters when available, and More; its bounded More dialog retains History, Bookmarks, Sleep, Mute and Volume. Expand, Popup and Close remain visible without scrolling. Phone and popup audiobook players retain the five primary tools. Mobile uses the edge-to-edge mini player, with at least 44px targets.

Expanded desktop: artwork occupies the left stage; large, generously spaced title, artist/contributors and album occupy the right stage. The persistent dock remains the transport and seek control surface; desktop Now Playing must not duplicate those controls. Audiobook book/chapter progress remains informational context. Reserve the attached workspace width before sizing the composition. Phone: centered art approximately 70–80vw bounded by height and width, music square and audiobook source ratio (contain), with centered identity beneath art. Phone title is 25–30px, artist/author 20px and narrator 16px, each on its own line. Audiobook Book Progress and Chapter progress are separate rows, followed by the chapter title, transport, and five circular tools; music uses four circular primary tools and compact secondary History. Primary play is 68–88px on phone; secondary transport 44–56px. The artwork's blurred color remains clearly visible through readable navy overlays. At 390×840 keep core controls reasonably within the viewport; on 320px let content scroll, keep 44px functional targets, and avoid horizontal overflow. Use safe-area padding.

Popup: 540–600px main stage with a 320–420px attached context panel, 20–24px corner radius, 20–28px inset. Center the combined composition in usable space, and center controls within the main stage. Audiobook art/identity can form a two-column upper area, timeline and transport beneath, tools on last row. Below about 900px stack the context panel. Request a centered named browser window; blocked launch leaves playback intact and offers the expanded player fallback.

The shipped popup size is 1040×780 so the first opening can use the attached-panel recipe; explicitly configured alternate dimensions remain supported. Clamp the requested geometry to the available screen. Audiobook History's media DurationSeconds is asset length. EndedAt minus StartedAt describes elapsed session time and may include pauses, so label it “Elapsed session”; invalid or missing intervals show a clearly labeled saved position instead. Music history includes only known music records, and audiobook history requires verified membership in the current book. Accepted audiobook history derives its displayed identity from the verified source asset and chapter timeline, including current source title overrides; a stale saved chapter string is cleared when no canonical chapter contains the saved position. It then displays the book and saved position without guessing a chapter.

Context rows: common horizontal inset 12–16px; leading slot 40px (art/state/ordinal/time), flexible title/subtitle, 48–64px duration, 36–44px action. History may use an 80px leading time slot. All labels/actions have explicit grid positions; never indent title by nesting whole rows in padded wrappers. Active row gets restrained shared accent fill and aria-current; blue queue bars animate only with IsPlaying and stop under prefers-reduced-motion. Group headers share row inset. Untimed lyrics have no fabricated active timing.

Shared components retain compatible parameters and gain explicit surface recipes (dock/expanded/phone/popup/video/panel) only where needed. Inline PlaybackToolSheet is a labeled section/region, not a modal dialog. Modal sheets use dialog semantics, focus containment/return and Escape close supplied by their host. Circular utilities expose text labels on full/mobile layouts; compact dock uses accessible icon labels. Progress and volume share track/thumb styling.

Control order belongs to the shared catalog's surface policy. Desktop expanded music context uses one compact row of Queue, Lyrics and History, with three equal 72px slots and 16px gaps. Phone/popup music primary tools are Queue, Lyrics, Shuffle, Repeat; History retains secondary access. The wide music dock uses compact Queue and Lyrics; when its actual width is constrained, More retains Queue, Lyrics, History, Mute and Volume while Expand, Popup and Close stay directly available. Video's reference row is Captions, Audio, Speed, Next Up and Fullscreen; supported Quality/Chapters retain secondary access. Unsupported capabilities are omitted, and secondary placement must not silently remove an available feature. Selection emphasizes the circular icon/value target, never a broad rectangular tool backing. Fractional playback rates remain exact in displayed values, including 1.25x and 1.75x.

Video: preserve host ownership and video sizing. The transport trio is an independent overlay centered at 50% of the stage width and height; desktop play is approximately 150px and skip circles 112px, separated by generous gaps. Identity sits in the lower-left above the full-width lower timeline. Settings follow below on the right in one primary row, using readable fixed slots with icons beside labels/current values. Secondary utilities retain separate compact access. The transport must not share the bottom toolbar. Narrow windows reduce the centered play/skip targets to approximately 80/56px and may scroll or wrap secondary choices without compressing labels into letters. Only real captions/audio/quality/Next Up choices render. View video applies the same centered shared transport and unboxed lower timeline within its own viewer stage, including when its information rail is open; its existing header, authorized resource handling and real browser track choices remain viewer-owned.

Video choices also include actual browser/HLS caption tracks when they are absent from the manifest/managed inventory, without duplicate rows. Selected/Off state reflects the actual track modes. Automatically positioned native cues reserve space above the visible lower controls; authored numeric cue placement is retained, and native PiP/restorable playback retains its original automatic placement. Library detail's bare video runtime numbers represent minutes and become explicit clock values at queue creation; Next Up omits unknown/nonpositive duration and never rounds a positive short duration to zero minutes.

## Session and identity contracts (WP1)

On a new playback request synchronously publish subject, Experience, PresentationSurface, queue/current index, reset position/error/gesture state, default media-specific settings, and valid panel/tab state before awaiting external settings/position/history/rate calls. Capture PlaybackRequestVersion and request token. Every asynchronous completion checks both version and subject before applying state or dispatching transport. Old completions must not notify obsolete capabilities, replace position/rate, load old history, or start old media. Follow the same rule for index/next/previous paths. Publish data completion separately. Existing request cancellation and host command path remain authoritative.

Responsive and dock-height observation start before remote settings finish. Each layout owns its observer registration and cleanup, so disposal of an older layout cannot remove a newer layout's observers or clear its live dock-height reservation. Expanded desktop context reserves at least the dock's safe bottom envelope and the dock remains above the context stage. Device changes publish their new presentation immediately; obsolete settings responses cannot restore an earlier device class.

Canonical destinations are derived from resolved media/entity IDs, never from display titles. Music title opens parent album detail (songs have no standalone detail); artists/contributors open resolved person details; audiobook opens its owned audiobook/media detail; playlist context opens the actual Listen playlist route. Queue and history items and serialized snapshots preserve these identities. Empty/unresolved IDs render plain text. Surface markup uses one shared route policy rather than reconstructing inconsistent links. Navigation does not stop or remount the audio host.
