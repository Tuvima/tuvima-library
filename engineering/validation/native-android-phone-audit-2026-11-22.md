# Android phone app audit against the Dashboard phone layout (WP3 of native-apps)

Status: Draft report, 2026-11-22. Read-only audit: no app code was changed. Spec: `engineering/specs/native-apps.md` (WP3, feeds WP8, WP9, WP10).

## In plain English

Shy, this compares the Android phone app (`clients/android/mobile` and the shared `clients/android/core`) with how the Dashboard looks and behaves on a phone, one screen at a time. It also lists which Engine connection points (the `/api/v1` actions the apps use) each screen needs and whether they exist.

The headline: the phone app is a solid browse-and-play app, but it is built around the old five-tab layout (Home, Libraries, Search, Downloads, Settings). The Dashboard's phone layout moved on in November 2026 to a three-tab floating dock (Home, Search, My List), a hamburger menu and section chips. My List does not exist in the app at all, although the Engine already has everything it needs. The Engine is in better shape than older notes said: Collections, Universe and View (photos) connection points all exist and the app already uses them. The real gaps are My List, the Read experience (no reader, no connection point under `/api/v1`), and the music and audiobook player (the phone has no music or audiobook screen of its own, only the system media player).

Words used: Engine (the server), Dashboard (the web app), Library, Series, Universe.

## How I read "built / partial / missing"

- **Built**: the screen exists and does what the Dashboard phone does.
- **Partial**: exists, but something the Dashboard has is absent.
- **Missing**: nothing in the app.
- Connection point: **exists** means the route is in `src/MediaEngine.Api/Endpoints`; **missing** means nothing under `/api/v1` does it.

## Connection points: what is missing under `/api/v1`

Already there (older notes are out of date): Collections (`/api/v1/collections`), Universe (`/api/v1/universe/{qid}`), View including People and Places, My List and Favorites and Reactions (`/api/v1/profile-state/...`), Recently added (`/display/recent`), the audiobook history, bookmarks and chapter overrides (`/api/v1/player/audiobooks/...`), playback, offline, text tracks, profile switch, pairing, activity.

Missing or unproven for the phone:

1. **Read** (books, PDF, comics): the reading actions live at `/read/...` and `/reader/...`, outside `/api/v1`. `/read` carries the `library.read` client scope but is not on the Dashboard's `/api/v1` pass-through, and `/reader` bookmarks and highlights carry no client scope. Needed only if Shy chooses a native reader.
2. **View, Collections, Universe**: not missing. People and Places exist but the app does not use them yet.
3. **A browser sign-in link for "Edit in browser"**: missing and a security question (above).
4. **Contracts fixtures**: no fixtures exist for My List (`profile-state`), `display/search`, `display/recent` or audiobook bookmarks. Spec rule: no new wire shape without Contracts types and fixtures, so each client read adds a fixture.
5. **Listen**: songs list, artists and Favorites need one check against `/display/browse` to see whether the Engine already returns them as lanes; I could not prove either way without running the Engine.

## Sizing of WP8, WP9 and WP10

- **WP8 (Missing connection points)**: smaller than the spec fears. Nothing is strictly missing for Home, My List, Libraries (except Read), Details and Search. Realistic scope: fixtures and Contracts tests for My List, search, recent, audiobook bookmarks; a small Listen check; and, only if Shy says yes, the Read routes under `/api/v1` and the browser sign-in link. Standard size stands; it shrinks to Mechanical if Read and the sign-in link are dropped.
- **WP9 (Browse and My List parity)**: Complex stands. Work: new shell (dock, menu, chips), My List page and plus/tick on tiles, hero and Details, Home My List row, Listen pages (songs, albums, playlists, artists, Favorites), View People and Places, Search overlay, Recently added, Details "..." menu. Suggest splitting in two proposed changes: 9a shell plus My List, 9b Listen and View additions.
- **WP10 (Playback parity and Edit in browser)**: the heaviest. The music Now playing view and the audiobook player do not exist and are bigger than the video work left. Work: a collapsed bar and full Now playing screen for music and audiobooks (queue, shuffle, repeat, lyrics, chapters, speed, sleep timer, bookmarks), contract lock-screen second line and actions, video Bookmarks and paused details, Edit in browser. Recommend splitting into 10a music and 10b audiobook, with video gaps and Edit in browser as 10c. It is Complex, probably two or three packets, not one.

2. Read on the phone in version 1: A. Open the Dashboard reader in the browser (recommended), B. Build a native reader (its own spec).
3. Edit in browser sign-in: A. Open the page and sign in there if asked (recommended), B. Add a one-time sign-in link from the Engine (a security change you must approve).

## Plain English summary

The Android phone app already does a lot well: pairing, profile switching, Watch lists, Collections, Universes, photos, video playback with subtitles and next-episode, downloads for offline use and Picture-in-Picture. The big holes are My List (nothing exists in the app, although the Engine fully supports it), a real Listen experience and music and audiobook player screens (today those play only through the system's notification), and the Dashboard's new three-tab phone layout. The Engine is mostly ready: Collections, Universe and View are not missing as older notes said. The only real Engine gaps are the Read routes (needed only if you want reading on the phone) and a way to sign in when "Edit in browser" opens the Dashboard. WP8 shrinks, WP9 should be split in two, and WP10 is the largest piece and should be split into music, audiobook and video-plus-edit.
