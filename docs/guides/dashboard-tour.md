# Find your way around

Take five minutes to find your media and tools in Tuvima Library. Start on Home, choose a [media lane](../reference/glossary.md#media-lane), or search for a title.

## Start on Home

Home shows a featured item and shelves such as Continue Across Media and Recently Added. Select a card for details. Use the featured action to start or resume it.

Recently Added offers All, Watch, Read, Listen, or View items you can access. It shows when media joined your library. A title may also appear on another Home shelf.

## Return to For Me

[For Me](for-me.md) gathers your Continue, My List, Favorites, Collections, Playlists, and Galleries. Empty shelves are left out. The items belong to your active profile.

My List saves items for later. For Me's Favorites currently shows songs marked with a heart. Continue follows your progress. Each choice is separate.

## Browse Read, Watch, and Listen

**Read** has books and comics. **Watch** has movies, TV shows, and movie series. **Listen** has music, audiobooks, and playlists.

1. Open a lane and browse its Discover shelves.
2. Choose a scope, such as **Books**, **TV Shows**, or **Music**, for the full browser.
3. Use search and the genre, person, year, and status filters where offered.
4. Choose a sort order, tile size, or Cards/List view where offered.
5. Open a card for details, then start the item.

Music starts in Songs view. Select a song to play it. Its menu can open the parent album. A TV show lists the episodes you own, not a provider's full episode catalog.

## Explore Collections

Collections offers **Discovery**, **Automatic**, **Curated**, **Shelves**, and **People**. Automatic groups connect related works. Curated holds groups someone has put together. Shelves lists eligible book, movie, and audiobook series. People lists primary contributors.

Your own collections also appear in For Me. A library collection enters My List only when you save it and have access. [Learn how groups work](../explanation/how-universes-work.md).

## Search the library

1. Select global **Search** or press **Ctrl+K**.
2. Enter a title, person, series, collection, or playlist.
3. Select a result, or choose **View all results** for the full Search page.

The search overlay opens above your current page. On the full page, narrow results by media, result type, year, and sort order.

## Open details and fix mistakes

Details shows the item's title, art, facts, and actions. Open **Edit** there to fix it, then return to the same page. [Correct an item](editing-items.md) explains which fields you can change.

## Open View

[View](view-personal-space.md) holds personal photos, videos, and other local media in a profile's Personal Space. Its menu has Photos, Folders, Galleries, People, and Places. Sharing follows your rights and the chosen scope.

## Check work and settings

The header activity icon opens **Operations** for system work you can access. Operations shows active work and recent batches. Review Queue holds items that need a human decision.

Settings holds folders, providers, profiles, playback choices, and admin tools. Your role controls which tools you see. [Library settings](library-settings.md) explains the admin tasks.
