## Make your library feel connected

Read a book, find an owned screen adaptation, open a creator's work or return to an unfinished audiobook. Trusted identities connect formats without treating outside catalog entries as files you own. Personal photos and other local files have their own View space.

- **Home** helps you discover, continue and find recent additions.
- **For Me** keeps your saved items, Favorites and personal collections together.
- **Read, Watch and Listen** organize browsing and playback by media lane.
- **View** holds personal media, folders, galleries and available people or location information.
- **Collections** brings related shelves and deliberate selections together.
- **Search** finds media, people and groups across the library.
- **Detail pages** explain an item and provide reading, playback and inline editing.
- **Review Queue** holds uncertain items that need human help.
- **Settings** manages libraries, providers, access, operations and system health.

[Take the Dashboard tour](guides/dashboard-tour.md) or [learn about Collections, Universes and shelves](explanation/how-universes-work.md).

## How it fits together

```text
Your folders
    ↓
Read file metadata → Identify the work → Enrich trusted identities
    ↓                                         ↓
Local catalog ← Organize owned media and relationships
    ↓
Dashboard → Browse · Search · Read · Watch · Listen
```

The Engine and catalog run on your host. Configured providers and other network-backed features can contact external services; see [privacy and local-first behavior](explanation/privacy-local-first.md).
